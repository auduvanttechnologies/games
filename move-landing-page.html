Why Muuuve

Not your average tic-tac-toe

Muuuve keeps the game everyone already knows, then rebuilds the scoring around a bigger board and a sharper idea: every line matters, not just the first one.

🎯

Every angle counts

Overlapping runs of three each score separately. A row of four isn't one win — it's two.

🧠

A Computer that thinks ahead

Choose Easy for a relaxed match, or Hard for an opponent that plans several moves out.

🪙

Face a friend

Local multiplayer with a coin flip to decide who opens — hand off the screen and play.

⚡

No install, just play

Move runs entirely in your browser. Open the link and you're already in the game.

How to Play

Three steps in

Muuuve takes seconds to learn, and the 5×5 board gives you a lot more room to think than the game you remember.

01

Choose your mode

Play solo against the Computer at your preferred difficulty, or set up a local match with a friend.

02

Take turns on the grid

Place your mark on the 5×5 board. Every empty cell is a chance to set up your next line.

03

Score every run of three

When the board fills, whoever completed the most runs — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal — wins.
