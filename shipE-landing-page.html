How a year works

The game lasts ten years. Every year follows the same loop.

  1. Check your company

    The left panel shows your cash, revenue, users, market share, reputation, and runway. The right panel shows the computer's value and your rank.

  2. Take up to 3 actions

    Pick from the action cards. Each costs cash and gives you something back. Cards turn grey when you can't afford them or when you're out of actions.

  3. End the year

    You have 2 minutes per year. Press End Year Early when you're done, or let the timer run out.

  4. Face the year's event

    Every year ends with an event. Most give you a choice, and the buttons show the cost or the odds. Then the year's expenses are paid and the next year begins.

How you win: after year 10, the company with the higher value wins. Cash on hand doesn't count toward your score, so spend it wisely, but don't run out.

What the numbers mean

Keep an eye on these. Some are your score, and some keep you alive.

Company value
What your company is worth. This decides who wins.
Cash
Money in the bank. Actions, events, and yearly expenses all draw it down.
Runway
How many months your cash would last at your current burn rate.
Burn rate
The cost of running the company each year, whether or not you take any actions. It starts small and grows a lot after Year 1.
Market share
The slice of your market you own. It slowly boosts your value.
Reputation
A 0 to 100 score. Higher means a small boost to value each year, and bigger funding rounds. Lower means the opposite.
Dilution
When you sell part of your company to investors, everyone who already owned shares owns a smaller percentage. You get cash, but you own less.

What can end your run

Bankruptcy. If your cash is negative at the end of two years in a row, creditors take over and the game ends. One bad year is a warning, so fix it quickly.
Treating investor money like your own. Personal spending is tempting, but your company remembers. It lowers your reputation now, and it can come back to hurt you years later.

Tips

  • Use all 3 actions most years. Investors reward momentum, and an idle year makes your next funding round smaller.
  • Check your runway before you spend. It's better to skip a card than to go negative.
  • Read the little hint under each event choice. It tells you what the choice costs or how risky it is.
  • Some choices help now and hurt later, and some do the opposite. Think about what could come up in the next few years.
  • The computer grows every year too, so standing still means falling behind.

Levels 1 and 2

These levels are about the road you choose.

The fork in the road

Answer the first question correctly to take the candy road. Get it wrong and you take the storm forest. Both roads are playable.

Candyland

Drag from each question to its matching answer. Right matches earn shields. Wrong ones cost health. Then answer questions to escape the quicksand.

The dark forest

Switch lanes with the up and down arrows to dodge thorns, answer questions to unlock Super Punch and Super Kick, then face the Skeleton King. Wait for the wind, then punch (Z) or kick (X) to block it, and attack in between.

Health

Your health bar starts full. Wrong answers cost health, and if it runs out you're out of the level.
