Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This is the Korvyn Games section of the Auduvant Technologies privacy policy. Korvyn Games is the games brand of Auduvant Technologies LLC ("we," "our," or "us"). This section covers:

  • the Korvyn Games website: the homepage at auduvanttechnologies.com/games/ and the pages about our games
  • our games Muuuve (auduvanttechnologies.com/muuuve/) and shipE (auduvanttechnologies.com/shipE/)

Everything in the Auduvant privacy policy applies here too. This section adds the details specific to our games. If the two ever differ, this section applies to Korvyn Games.

At a glance

  • There are no accounts. You don't sign up, log in or type in anything about yourself.
  • Our games save nothing in your browser and send nothing about how you play.
  • We count visitors without cookies and without storing IP addresses. All we keep is how many people visited each page on each day.
  • Our games will never show ads. If ads come to the other pages of this site, only visitors who tell us they are 18 or older will see them.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

1. Playing our games

Muuuve and shipE run entirely in your browser. You don't need an account and you are never asked for your name, email address or anything else about yourself.

  • The games don't use cookies or store anything in your browser. Your progress is not saved: when you close or reload the page, the game starts over.
  • The games don't send your moves, answers, scores or choices to us or to anyone else.
  • shipE plays background music from a file on our own website. You can mute it at any time.

2. Counting visitors

We want to know how many people use our games, so each page on this site and each game tells our visit counter its own name when it opens (for example, "muuuve"). Nothing else is sent by the page.

Like every website, the counter receives your IP address and your browser type (the "user agent" your browser sends with every request). This is what happens to them:

  • The counter combines your IP address and browser type with a secret code that changes every day, and turns them into a one-way code. The code can't be turned back into your IP address. This happens in the counter's memory; your IP address and browser type are never written down.
  • The one-way code is kept only until the end of that day (midnight, Coordinated Universal Time), so that you are counted once per page per day, however many times you visit.
  • Shortly after midnight UTC, the counter adds up the day's totals, then deletes all of that day's codes and that day's secret.
  • What we keep is one number for each page for each day, plus the number of different visitors to the whole site that day. These numbers contain no personal information.

The counter does not use cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Because the secret changes every day, the counter can't tell whether you visited yesterday, can't follow you to other websites, and can't build a profile of you. We don't use it for ads, and we can't use it to identify or contact you. We have switched off the request logs that would otherwise record IP addresses. Search engines and other automated visitors are not counted.

The counter runs on Google Cloud in the United States. Our legal reason under the GDPR is our legitimate interest in knowing how many people use our games, which we meet with the least information possible.

3. Hosting

This site and our games are hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads a page, and uses it to deliver and secure the site under the GitHub privacy statement. We do not receive or keep that information.

Our fonts and all other files come from our own website. Apart from GitHub and our visit counter, your browser does not contact any other company when you use this site or our games.

4. Advertising

Muuuve and shipE will never show ads.

The other pages of this site do not show ads today. In the future they may show ads from Zawadi Media, our own advertising service. When they do:

  • Before any ad is shown, we will ask for your year of birth once. The question won't suggest an answer or say which ages see ads.
  • If you are under 18, or you choose not to answer, you will never see an ad on this site.
  • Your answer is saved only in your own browser, so you aren't asked on every visit. It is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's data for this site.
  • Ads come from Zawadi Media, which uses no cookies, builds no profiles and doesn't keep your IP address, as its privacy section explains.

We will update this page before ads start.

5. Children

Anyone may play our games, and we collect no personal information from players of any age. Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13.

The visit counter, described in section 2, is used only to count visitors, which supports the running of our site. Its one-way code can't be used to identify or contact anyone, is never used for ads or profiles, and is deleted at the end of each day. Ads, when they start, will be shown only to visitors who tell us they are 18 or older.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for advertising. The only companies involved in running Korvyn Games are GitHub, which hosts the site, and Google Cloud, which runs our visit counter.

7. Your rights

You have the rights described in section 7 of the Auduvant privacy policy, wherever you live. Because our games keep no personal information about players, we will usually have nothing to give you, correct or delete, other than emails you have sent us. You can still ask us at any time.

8. Changes to this section

When we change how our games or this site handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
