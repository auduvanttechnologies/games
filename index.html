How we build

A few principles guide everything we ship — games included.

01

Simple and elegant

We'd rather a game be easy to learn and hard to put down than complicated for its own sake.

02

Visually satisfying

Every completed move should feel good — clean animation and feedback, without unnecessary noise.

03

Built to replay

Good games hold up on the tenth play, not just the first. We design for the long game.
